## About

**SudokuGen** is a web app by New Media XP ("NMX", "we", "us") for creating, solving, playing and sharing sudoku puzzles, at <https://www.newmediaxp.com/app/sudokugen>. This policy covers SudokuGen in your browser, when you install it from your browser, and in any app store where we offer it.

NMX is an independent developer, not a registered company. You can reach us at the contact details at the end of this page.

---

## Information We Collect

None. SudokuGen works entirely on your device. It has no account, no ads and no analytics, and we don't sell or share anything about you.

---

## What's Saved On Your Device

SudokuGen uses your browser's own storage on your device. None of it is sent to us.

- **Your current puzzle**, so you can carry on later: the puzzle, your entries and notes, the cells you revealed, and the size and number of blanks you chose.
- **Your light or dark theme**, if you choose one on our website.
- **An offline copy of SudokuGen**, so it opens without an internet connection.

To remove them, clear the site data for `www.newmediaxp.com` in your browser's settings.

---

## Sharing Puzzles

- A puzzle's share link holds only the puzzle's code, and its seed number when SudokuGen knows it. It holds nothing about you.
- If you share through Facebook, Threads, X, LinkedIn, Reddit or Pinterest, that site opens in a new tab with the link filled in, and that site's privacy policy applies. Your device's share sheet works the same way, with the app you choose.
- Puzzle codes you paste in, including links from Shaolin Sudoku, are read only on your device.

## Technical Information

SudokuGen is part of our website, which Cloudflare hosts. When your browser loads SudokuGen, Cloudflare receives the request, including your IP address and browser details, to deliver the page and protect the site from attacks. Cloudflare may keep logs of requests for a short time, under its own privacy policy. We see only totals, such as the number of visits, not who visited.

SudokuGen sets no cookies. Cloudflare may set its own cookie to protect the site from bots and attacks.

Cloudflare's privacy policy: <https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/>
