## Information We Collect

The website doesn't ask for or collect personal information. It has no sign-up, no forms, no ads and no analytics, and we don't sell or share anything about you.

**Technical information**

- Cloudflare hosts the website. When your browser loads a page, Cloudflare receives the request, including your IP address and browser details, to deliver the page and protect the site from attacks. Cloudflare may keep logs of requests for a short time, under its own privacy policy. We see only totals, such as the number of visits, not who visited.

**Cookies and browser storage**

- The website sets no cookies. Cloudflare may set its own cookie to protect the site from bots and attacks.
- If you choose a light or dark theme, your browser saves that choice on your device. It isn't sent to us.
- Our pages load their fonts, images and code from our own site, not from other companies.

**Emails you send us**

- When you email us, we receive your email address, your name if your email shows it, and what you write. We use them only to answer you, and keep them for as long as we need to for that.
- Cloudflare forwards email sent to `contact@newmediaxp.com` to our mailbox, which Google (Gmail) hosts.

Privacy policies of these companies:

- Cloudflare: <https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/>
- Google: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>
